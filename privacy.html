SellWare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

SellWare does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you type into the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

The products, prices, costs, and settings you enter are saved only in your device's local storage, so the app can show them to you next time you open it. This data never leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server operated by CentWare that receives it.

What we collect

Nothing. SellWare has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data collection. The app works fully offline.

Sharing

Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared, sold, or disclosed to anyone. If you use the app's Export feature, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

SellWare is a general business tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
