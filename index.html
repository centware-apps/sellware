Install on iPhone

Open SellWare
  1. Open this page in Safari (the blue compass). Other apps’ built-in browsers can’t install it.
  2. Tap Open SellWare above.
  3. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  5. Open SellWare from your Home Screen icon from now on, so your data stays in one place.
Apple doesn’t allow downloading apps from outside the App Store, so on iPhone SellWare installs this way. It looks and works like an app, opens full screen and works with no internet.

Install on Android

⬇ Download SellWare.apk
  1. Tap Download SellWare.apk above (310 KB).
  2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone may ask to allow installs from the browser — that's normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install, then open SellWare from your app drawer or Home Screen.
The real Android app — no store, no account, no ads. Works on Android 10 and up, and works with no internet. Prefer not to install files? Open the web app in Chrome and use the ⋮ menu → Install app instead.
Your data lives only on your phone. Your saved products are stored on this device only — use Export inside SellWare to keep a backup copy, since saved products can’t be recovered if you delete the app.
